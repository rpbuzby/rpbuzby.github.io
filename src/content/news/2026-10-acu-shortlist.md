---
title: "Four poems shortlisted for the ACU Prize for Poetry 2026"
date: 2026-10-08
---
All four poems I entered made the shortlist: ‘Antigone’, ‘Cenotaph’, ‘Statement’ and ‘Veronica’. The prize, run by Australian Catholic University since 2013, is one of the country’s richest for a single poem. This year’s theme was ‘Witness’, drawn from Elie Wiesel’s words: ‘For the dead and the living, we must bear witness.’ The poems will appear in the prize anthology. The winners are named at a presentation in Sydney on 5 November.
