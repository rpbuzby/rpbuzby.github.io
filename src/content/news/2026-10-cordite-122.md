title: "‘Deed of release’ accepted for Cordite Poetry Review Issue 122"
date: 2026-10-14

Thabani Tshuma and Georgia Kartas, the guest poetry editors for Cordite 122, Thrum, have taken ‘Deed of release’ for the issue, which publishes in November 2026. My second poem with Cordite, after ‘The singing, not the song’ in Issue 121.
