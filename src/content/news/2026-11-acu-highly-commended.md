One of five highly commended, named at the university’s Prizes and Awards Presentation in Sydney on 5 November. The judges, Margot Hillel and Robert H. F. Carver, chose 118 poems by 88 poets for the prize anthology, Witness, which is out now and carries all four of the poems I entered.
